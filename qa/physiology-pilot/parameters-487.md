Independent expectations test **directions over windows**, plus label-based cessation/delayed response. They do not copy exact simulator vital-sign deltas. A rejected all-case expectation is recorded: interstitial fluid removal is not guaranteed during active hemorrhage/capillary loss; the CIRR decongestion envelope is conditional on its stable fluid state. The model must not be promoted until clinician-reviewed magnitudes, timing, uncertain latent states and device measurements pass.
